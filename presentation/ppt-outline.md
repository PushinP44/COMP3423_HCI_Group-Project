# PPT Outline

*Owner: Member 4 (Visual & UI Designer). Rule: screenshot-driven, no walls of text — each slide should be readable in ~5 seconds.*

1. **Title** — project title, team, app being redesigned.
2. **Problem & users** — who's affected, the core problem statement (1-2 slides).
3. **User journey** — the illustrated journey with pain points highlighted.
4. **Design overview** — the 5 features at a glance, overall design principles applied.
5. **Feature 1-5** — one slide each: before/after screenshot, HCI principle named, one-line rationale.
6. **Testing method** — who, how many, what tasks (1 slide).
7. **Results** — key numbers + 1-2 standout quotes.
8. **Iteration** — what changed after testing (before/after of the change itself).
9. **Conclusion & future work** — 1 slide.

Keep total slide count reasonable for a ≤10 min video — roughly 1 slide per 30-45 seconds of talking, so ~15-18 slides max.
